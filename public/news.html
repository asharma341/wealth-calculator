A beginner’s filter

Ask three questions before reacting.

1

Is it new?

Markets often move before a headline reaches you. Do not assume “breaking” means an easy opportunity.

2

Does it change the long term?

A daily price move matters less than a lasting change in earnings, inflation, rates, debt or geopolitical risk.

3

Does it change my plan?

Act because your goal, horizon or risk capacity changed—not because a dramatic headline made you anxious.

News may be delayed, incomplete or updated after publication. Verify important decisions with the original source.
